## Design Binding

- **Stable identity**: existing `ArtifactReference`; do not change artifact-manifest-v2 identity or original Data names.
- **Lifecycle owner**: one resolved typed mode per app API/service registration; In-App local durable data is app-owned and is not a remote replica; Persistent service owns remote durability/replication.
- **Historical facts**: per-Repo `RepoWriteReceipt` plus immutable aggregate `ArtifactCommitReceipt`; only unique durable commits count.
- **Current facts**: `ArtifactLocationRecord` fetched directly from its owning Repo over the existing STATUS/LOCATION operation and a bounded `AvailabilityReport`, not receipt mutation or a forwarded hint snapshot.
- **Fetch state**: operation-local normalized authenticated `providerIdentity` visited set, artifact/location-version cooldown, one monotonic global deadline; each attempt also records its distinct logical `repoNode`; no auth/integrity downgrade.
- **DI boundary**: DI owns model/input/output semantics and decides what is worth retaining; Repo stores opaque object bytes/manifests; no automatic KV/activation/session replication.

Detailed field and public method semantics are normative in [data-model.md](data-model.md) and [contracts/repo-api.md](contracts/repo-api.md). Trace every public export/config loader to its actual backend and every backend through registered service/production target; do not introduce a helper that production does not call.
