# T003 Wire Contract Evidence

Date: 2026-08-22
Branch: `Experimental`

## Scope

T003 closes the version-1 streamed invocation wire/name contract only. It does
not claim a streamed lifecycle, ONNX execution, MiniNDN, SIF, or Tiger result.

Implemented evidence:

- `InvocationStream.hpp/.cpp` owns the collision-cleared contiguous TLVs
  `0xF661..0xF683`, strict nested canonical decoding, checked narrowing, exact
  event names, binding/transcript digests, nonce/AAD derivation, and completion
  matching. `HybridMessageEnvelope` now has an explicit `STREAM-GRANT` form
  that carries only the recipient-wrapped key; this is a wire representation,
  not the T007 ProviderGrant authorization binding.
- `RequestMessage` carries at most one final `StreamRequestOptions` block and
  `ResponseMessage` carries at most one final `StreamCompletion` block. Both
  paths copy, clear, encode, decode, and reject duplicate/non-final blocks.
- The canonical binding byte framing is documented in
  `contracts/wire-protocol-v1.md` and fixed digest/wire vectors are asserted.
