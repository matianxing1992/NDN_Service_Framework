## Coverage and interpretation

The trace reported at-least-one-Provider coverage of 93.2% at 110 m and 97.3%
at 120 m; all-Provider outage fractions were 6.8% and 2.7%. Thus, increasing
coverage does not monotonically improve NDNSF relative performance in this
implementation. The 110 m condition is approximately tied, while the 120 m
condition is strongly negative for NDNSF despite the lower Provider execution
count.

No 3-seed confirmation was authorized: neither range produced a positive
screen signal. The result is `NO_DEMONSTRATED_ADVANTAGE` for this boundary
screen.
