# Coverage Sweep: 70/80/90 m at 15 m/s (2026-08-06)

## Purpose

This follow-up checks whether the lower-coverage trend changes between 50 m
and 100 m. It keeps the primary one-AP/four-Provider topology, blocked-network
coverage gate, 1 s NDNSF ACK timeout, 1 s sequential gRPC/NSC attempt timeout,
5 RPS, and 60-second measured windows.

The 70/80/90 m ranges are secondary extensions. The default primary matrix
remains 50/100 m; these ranges are selected only with explicit `--ranges`.

outlier, not evidence of a stable NDNSF advantage.

## Trace coverage and conclusion

For 90 m, the fraction of epochs with at least one Provider in range was
84.0%, 85.9%, and 74.5% for seeds 40/41/42; all-Provider outage fractions were
16.0%, 14.1%, and 25.5%. Thus this is not an all-outage-dominated condition,
yet the three-seed aggregate still does not favor NDNSF.

The result is `NO_DEMONSTRATED_ADVANTAGE` for the 70/80/90 m sweep. Higher
baseline retry counts alone are insufficient: NDNSF's lower Provider execution
count (0.731/request versus roughly 2.5 baseline attempts/request) does not
guarantee higher terminal success. No paper claim should be added from the
single-seed 90 m screen.
