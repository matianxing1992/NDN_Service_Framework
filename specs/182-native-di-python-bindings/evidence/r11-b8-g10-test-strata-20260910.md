## 0.6B and MiniNDN boundaries

The repository contains separate Qwen3-0.6B workloads, including Spec163/170 placement
and validation material. Those records may describe CPU reference generation or MiniNDN
execution for their own Spec; they are not evidence for the Spec182 `Spec182*`, G7, G9 or
R10-B73 results.

MiniNDN is a separate Spec182 T016 qualification layer. R10-B47/ R10-B48 provide bounded
PO-001 and I01 native owner/consumer passes, but the complete I01-I08 and PO-001-PO-014
matrix, maintained callers, no-Python proof and final T016 gate remain open. No current
`Spec182*` count should be reported as a MiniNDN result.
