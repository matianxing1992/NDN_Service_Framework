```text
T001 -> T002 -> T003
          |       |
          |       +-> T004 --------------------+
          +----------> T005                    |
                  T006 -> T007 ----------------+
                                                v
                                         T008 -> T009
                                                |
                                                v
                                         T010 -> T011
                                                |
                                                v
                                         T012 -> T013
```

- T005 can proceed after T002 while T003/T004 are being completed because it
  owns only external loading and compatibility.
- T006 can proceed after T002/T003 and is independent of T004/T005 source
  ownership.
- T007 depends on T006.
- T008 depends on a sealed plan from T004 and a usable strategy/catalog path.
- T009 depends on T008.
- T010/T011 depend on the final-Selection and orthogonal role-readiness seams;
  their acceptance must not assume that all roles are locally ready before
  execution begins.
- T012/T013 are integration gates, not permission for live model work.

## Implementation Strategy

### MVP

T001–T005 constitute the planning MVP:

```text
validated candidates + validated provider ACKs
  -> replaceable joint strategy
  -> sealed automatic collaboration plan
```

Stop and audit that boundary before adding preparation side effects.

### Incremental delivery

1. T001–T005: automatic replaceable planning.
2. T006–T007: pre-split operator API and default policy.
3. T008–T009: signed ACK offers, exact Selection, and role-readiness lifecycle.
4. T010–T011: event-driven DAG execution and compensation.
5. T012–T013: local Docker, MiniNDN, docs, and evidence closure.
6. Only then complete the later-added Spec 162 T009 requalification and seek
   new live authorization.

## Fragmentation scan

- Contract tests, implementation, focused validation, and evidence are
  intentionally coalesced inside each behavioral task.
- T006/T007 remain separate because catalog mutation authority and placement
  policy are independently reviewable and security-distinct.
- T008/T009 remain separate because Core ACK/Selection authorization and DI
  assignment/capacity/readiness integration carry different risks. They share
  two acceptance invariants: final Selection assigns only roles within the
  signed ACK offer, and no role executes before its own Selection,
  local-readiness, and authenticated-input latches are satisfied.
- T010/T011 remain separate because generic dataflow correctness is
  independently useful without recovery optimization; T010 consumes the
  role-readiness seam from T009 rather than assuming a globally prepared plan.
- T012/T013 remain separate because local-container closure is a prerequisite
  for the network/failure matrix.
