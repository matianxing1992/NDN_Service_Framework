\section*{Interpretation limits}
This is an automated \textbf{word-level comparison}, not a semantic audit.
Exact passages of at least 12 words are also matched across reordered sections.
Other moved or reworded passages may appear on both sides; a red mark does not
establish that a research topic was discarded. Ligatures and line-end
hyphenation are normalized for matching. Title, contents and page-number-only
changes are not highlighted. Figures and tables remain in their original
positions; changes inside graphical content are not automatically marked.
Both source PDFs and the clean current manuscript are unchanged.
