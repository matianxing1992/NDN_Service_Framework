\begin{frame}{Current Solution: Fixed Camera}
\begin{block}{Why start here?}
Simpler hardware; constant camera-to-body geometry.
\end{block}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Calibrate:} camera intrinsics and the fixed camera-to-body transform.
\item \textbf{Capture:} reposition the UAV; prefer low-motion intervals and short exposures when feasible.
\item \textbf{Record:} pair each image with capture-time position, attitude and timestamp.
\item \textbf{Analyze:} estimate viewing direction from attitude and calibration; verify same-object matches before combining views.
\end{itemize}
\begin{alertblock}{Limitation}
Attitude estimates do not prevent blur or jitter. Reject poor frames.
\end{alertblock}
\evidence{Implementation plan; calibration and timing require validation. Metadata reference: \href{https://mavlink.io/en/messages/common.html\#CAMERA_IMAGE_CAPTURED}{MAVLink capture metadata}.}
